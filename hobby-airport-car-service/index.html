Hobby vs IAH

Hobby Airport Quick Guide

Everything you need to know about flying out of William P. Hobby Airport.

Southwest Airlines Hub

Hobby is Southwest's primary Houston hub with nonstop service to over 60 destinations nationwide. Concourses serve all gates efficiently.

Two Concourses

Hobby has two concourses connected post-security. The main terminal houses ticketing, baggage claim, and ground transportation on the lower level.

Easy In, Easy Out

Hobby is known for its quick security lines (avg 10-15 min with TSA PreCheck) and short walks to gates. From curb to gate in under 20 minutes.

Cruise Connections

Many cruise travelers fly into Hobby for Galveston cruise departures. We offer direct Hobby-to-Galveston transfers with group vehicle options.

FAQ

Hobby Airport Car Service Questions

Which airlines operate at Hobby Airport?▼
Hobby Airport primarily serves Southwest Airlines, Delta Air Lines, and American Airlines. Southwest operates the majority of flights from Hobby's concourses. We service all airline arrivals and departures.
How long does it take from Downtown to Hobby Airport?▼
Downtown Houston to Hobby Airport takes approximately 10-15 minutes via I-45 South. We recommend departing 2 hours before domestic flights. Since Hobby is primarily domestic, you need less buffer time than IAH.
Do you offer meet and greet at Hobby?▼
Yes, our chauffeur meets you inside baggage claim with a professional name sign. We track your flight and time our arrival so we're there when you land. We assist with luggage and escort you to your vehicle curbside.
How does Hobby compare to IAH for car service?▼
Hobby is smaller and closer to downtown Houston (10-15 min vs 25-35 min for IAH). It primarily serves domestic routes, mainly Southwest Airlines. Hobby is more convenient for travelers from south Houston, Pearland, and Galveston.
Can you accommodate last-minute Hobby Airport pickups?▼
Yes, we accept same-day bookings subject to availability. Call (832) 567-8050 for immediate service — we'll dispatch the nearest available vehicle.
Do you provide Hobby to Galveston cruise transfers?▼
Yes, we offer dedicated Hobby to Galveston cruise port transfers. Our Escalade and Sprinter vans are perfect for cruise groups with luggage. We monitor your flight arrival and coordinate with cruise check-in times.
