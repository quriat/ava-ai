FAQ

Wedding Limo Questions

How far in advance should I book wedding transportation?▼
We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for weddings, especially during peak season (March-June and September-November). Popular vehicles like the Mercedes S-Class and Escalade book quickly for wedding dates.
Can you accommodate multiple wedding locations?▼
Absolutely. Our hourly charter service is perfect for multi-stop wedding days — getting ready location to ceremony to reception. Same vehicle and chauffeur throughout the day with coordinated timing between locations.
What vehicles do you recommend for a wedding party?▼
For the bride & groom: Mercedes S-Class (up to 3). For the bridal party: Cadillac Escalade (up to 6). For larger groups: Mercedes Sprinter (up to 14). Many couples book multiple vehicles.
Do you offer red carpet service at wedding venues?▼
Yes, we offer white glove service including door-to-door assistance, professional chauffeurs in formal attire, and coordinated arrivals. Your chauffeur will open doors, assist with attire, and ensure photo-worthy arrivals.
Can you transport wedding decorations and attire?▼
Yes, our vehicles have ample space for wedding attire, decorations, gifts, and personal items. The Escalade and Sprinter are especially well-suited for transporting dresses, suits, floral arrangements, and décor.
What is your cancellation policy for wedding bookings?▼
Full refunds for cancellations 30+ days before the wedding. 50% refund for 14-30 days. Cancellations within 14 days may be charged up to the full amount. Contact us as soon as possible to discuss options.
