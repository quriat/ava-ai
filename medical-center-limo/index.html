FAQ

Medical Transportation Questions

Do you provide transportation to all Texas Medical Center hospitals?▼
Yes, we serve all major hospitals in the Texas Medical Center including MD Anderson Cancer Center, Memorial Hermann Hospital, Houston Methodist Hospital, Baylor St. Luke's Medical Center, Texas Children's Hospital, and Harris Health System. We also serve outpatient clinics and medical offices in the TMC area.
Can you accommodate wheelchair transportation?▼
While our standard Mercedes S-Class and Cadillac Escalade fleet is not wheelchair accessible, we can arrange accessible transportation through our partner network with advance notice. Please contact us at (832) 567-8050 at least 48 hours before your appointment to arrange wheelchair-accessible service.
Do you offer medical appointment reminders?▼
Yes! We can set up appointment reminders via text or email. When you book with AvaLimo, let us know your appointment time, and we'll send you a reminder 24 hours and 2 hours before pickup. We recommend scheduling your pickup 30-45 minutes before your appointment to allow for traffic and check-in time.
Can you wait during long medical appointments?▼
Absolutely. We understand medical appointments can vary in length. We offer both round-trip service with wait time, or you can book separate pickups. For chemotherapy, dialysis, or same-day surgery appointments, we recommend our hourly service so your chauffeur is ready when you are. We also track your phone so we can adjust pickup times if your appointment runs long.
Do you transport medical equipment?▼
Yes, our vehicles can accommodate medical equipment such as walkers, portable oxygen concentrators, and mobility aids. Our Mercedes Sprinter van offers extra space for larger equipment. Please inform us when booking if you're traveling with medical equipment so we can ensure proper vehicle selection.
Is your service available for early morning medical appointments?▼
Yes! We operate 24/7 and regularly transport patients to early morning appointments, surgeries, and procedures. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance for early morning pickups (before 6 AM) to guarantee availability. Our chauffeurs are trained to provide quiet, comfortable rides so you arrive relaxed for your appointment.
