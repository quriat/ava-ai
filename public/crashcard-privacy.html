CrashCard — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

CrashCard ("the app") helps you scan and organize insurance and driver's license information at accident scenes, published by Adam J. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

None leaves your device. CrashCard does not transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no cloud storage, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. All scanning and text recognition (OCR) happens on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

Photos you take, the card details extracted from them, incident records, and officer information you enter are stored locally on your device only. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you scan a card or take a damage photo.
  • Location (approximate) — if granted, used to tag an incident record with where it happened. Stored on your device only.
  • Fingerprint / device unlock — if you enable app lock, used only to unlock the app locally. No biometric data is accessed or stored by the app.

You can decline or revoke any permission at any time in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
