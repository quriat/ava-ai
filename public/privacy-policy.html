Privacy Policy

AvaLimo — Houston, Texas · Effective October 30, 2026

AvaLimo (“we”, “us”) operates the website avalimo.net and provides luxury chauffeur and airport transfer services in the Houston, Texas area. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

When you request a quote or book a ride, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, pickup and drop-off locations, flight information, and any notes you add about your trip. If you pay by card, payment is processed by our payment provider (Square); we do not store full card numbers on our systems.

Our website uses basic analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual visitors.

How we use your information

We use your information only to provide and improve our services: confirming and performing your rides, communicating with you about your booking, sending receipts, and responding to your requests. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Google user data and the AvaLimo Uploader

AvaLimo Uploader is an internal tool we operate to publish our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It connects to Google services using the YouTube Data API with the account owner’s permission.

The tool accesses only the YouTube data needed to upload videos and confirm they posted (the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes). It does not collect, store, sell, or share Google user data beyond completing those uploads. Access tokens are stored securely on our own systems, are used solely for this purpose, and can be revoked at any time from the Google account’s third-party access settings.

AvaLimo Uploader’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and security

We keep booking records only as long as needed to run our business and meet legal obligations. We take reasonable measures to protect the information in our care, including access controls on the systems where it is stored.
