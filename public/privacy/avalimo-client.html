AvaLimo (Client App) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

The AvaLimo app ("the app") is the booking app for AvaLimo, a luxury chauffeur service operated by AVALIMO LLC of Missouri City, Texas. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

The app has no user accounts and no sign-up. The only personal information the app collects is what you type into the booking form when you request a ride:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Pickup and drop-off locations
  • Date and time of the ride, passenger and luggage counts, and vehicle choice
  • Flight number (optional) and any special instructions you add

When you submit the form, these details are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to AvaLimo's own booking system so our dispatch team can arrange and fulfil your ride. That is the only purpose for which they are used.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your ride history ("My Rides") is stored locally on your phone only, so your details are ready for your next booking. It is not transmitted to us. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking. Booking details go only to AvaLimo's own booking system, operated by AVALIMO LLC.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. If you tap the call or WhatsApp shortcuts, your phone's own dialer or the WhatsApp app opens — anything you do there is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is a ride-booking service intended for adults arranging transportation. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
