AvaLimo Driver — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

AvaLimo Driver ("the app") is the working app for chauffeurs who drive for AvaLimo, a luxury chauffeur service operated by AVALIMO LLC of Missouri City, Texas. It is a work tool for AvaLimo's own drivers. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Trip Data the App Receives

The app downloads AvaLimo's trip feed from AvaLimo's own dispatch system so the assigned driver can run their trips. A trip entry shows the client name, trip date and time, pickup and drop-off addresses, flight number, and the client's phone number. This is work data provided by AvaLimo dispatch, displayed only to the driver the trip is assigned to.

Location — Only When You Tap

The app does not track your location in the background, and it never collects your location continuously. Your location is requested at one moment only: when you tap a trip status button (such as On My Way, Arrived, Picked Up, or Completed). At that moment, the app sends your current location together with that status update to AvaLimo dispatch, so dispatch knows where the update came from. Location is not collected at any other time.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores locally on your phone: the name you picked on first launch, the most recently downloaded trip list (so you can still see your runs with poor signal), and the last status you reported per trip. This data stays on your device; if you delete the app, it is deleted with it.

Data Sharing

Status updates and the location attached to them go only to AvaLimo's own dispatch system. We do not sell information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking.

Accounts

The app has no sign-up and no password. On first launch the driver picks their own name from the driver list so dispatch can attribute status updates.
