FAQ

Corporate Car Service Questions

Do you offer corporate accounts with invoicing?▼
Yes, we offer corporate accounts with net-30 invoicing, monthly statements, and detailed ride reporting. Contact adam@avalimo.net to establish your account — we can onboard new corporate clients within 24 hours.
Can you handle airport transfers for corporate travelers?▼
Absolutely. We provide executive airport transfers for IAH and Hobby with flight tracking, meet & greet, and luggage assistance. Professional greeting at baggage claim with seamless door-to-door service.
What areas of Houston do you serve for corporate transportation?▼
We cover all major business districts: Downtown, Galleria/Uptown, Energy Corridor, Texas Medical Center, Greenway Plaza, Midtown, Memorial, West University, Bellaire, The Woodlands, Sugar Land, Katy, and both airports. We also serve out-of-town destinations for executive road trips.
What vehicles are available for corporate travel?▼
Our executive fleet includes the Mercedes S-Class (up to 3), Cadillac Escalade (up to 6), and Mercedes Sprinter (up to 14). All vehicles are immaculately maintained and equipped with Wi-Fi.
Do you offer hourly corporate charters?▼
Yes, hourly corporate charters start at $120/hour for sedans and SUVs, $190/hour for Sprinter, with a 2-hour minimum. Perfect for multi-stop business days, client entertainment, and conferences. Same vehicle and chauffeur throughout.
Can you accommodate last-minute corporate bookings?▼
Yes, same-day bookings available for corporate clients. Corporate account holders get priority dispatch. Call (832) 567-8050 for immediate service.
