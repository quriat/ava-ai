FAQ

Prom Night Questions

How far in advance should I book a prom limo?▼
We recommend booking 4-6 weeks before prom night. Prom season (April-June) books up quickly, especially for Friday and Saturday nights. Call (832) 567-8050 to check last-minute availability.
What vehicle is best for a prom group?▼
The Mercedes Sprinter is the #1 choice for prom groups — seats up to 14 with high ceilings, ambient lighting, and premium sound. For smaller groups of 4-6, the Cadillac Escalade is popular. For couples, the Mercedes S-Class offers an elegant arrival.
How long is a typical prom rental?▼
Most prom rentals are 4-5 hours, covering pre-prom photos, dinner, the prom event, and post-prom activities. Hourly charter starts at $120/hour for sedans and SUVs, $190/hour for Sprinter, with a 3-hour minimum for prom bookings.
Can we bring music and connect our phones?▼
Yes! All vehicles have Bluetooth and auxiliary connectivity. Stream your own prom playlist through the premium sound system. The Sprinter also has customizable ambient lighting to match prom colors.
Is the prom limo safe for students?▼
Absolutely. All chauffeurs are professionally licensed, background-checked, and trained in defensive driving. Vehicles undergo regular safety inspections. Strict no-alcohol, no-smoking policy for all student bookings.
Can we customize the route for prom photos?▼
Yes, we plan photo stops into your itinerary. Popular spots include downtown Houston skyline views, Discovery Green, Memorial Park, and Rice University. Your chauffeur knows the best photo locations near your venue.
