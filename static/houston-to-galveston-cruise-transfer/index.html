FAQ

Cruise Transfer Questions

How long does it take from Houston to the Galveston cruise port?▼
Travel time from IAH is approximately 75-90 minutes. From Hobby it's about 45-55 minutes. From downtown Houston about 50-60 minutes. We recommend arriving at the cruise port by 1:00 PM for embarkation.
Which cruise terminals in Galveston do you serve?▼
We serve all Galveston cruise terminals: Terminal 1 (Royal Caribbean at Pier 25), Terminal 2 (Carnival at Pier 24), and Terminal 3 (Disney, Norwegian, and others at Pier 27).
Can you handle large groups with luggage for a cruise?▼
Absolutely. Our Mercedes Sprinter van seats up to 14 passengers with massive cargo space for cruise luggage. For larger groups we can arrange multiple vehicles. Book at least 2 weeks in advance for peak cruise dates.
Do you offer round-trip cruise transfers?▼
Yes, we offer round-trip transfers. We'll pick you up at the cruise port on disembarkation day and take you back to IAH, Hobby, or your hotel. We monitor ship arrival times to ensure punctual pickup.
What if my flight is delayed on cruise day?▼
We monitor your flight in real-time and adjust pickup automatically. Our chauffeurs coordinate with your schedule to ensure you reach the port in time. We recommend booking flights that arrive by noon on embarkation day.
Do you offer child car seats for cruise transfers?▼
Yes, we provide car seats at no extra charge. Please specify the number, type, and ages of children when booking so we can have them properly installed before your pickup.
