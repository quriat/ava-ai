FAQ

IAH Airport Car Service Questions

Do you track my flight for IAH pickups?▼
Yes, we monitor your flight in real-time from the moment you depart. We adjust for delays, early arrivals, and gate changes — all automatically. You never need to call us about flight status.
Where do you meet me at IAH?▼
Our chauffeur meets you at baggage claim with a name sign. For Terminal A, we meet near baggage carousel 4. Terminal B near carousel 1. Terminal C near carousel 3. Terminal D/E near the international arrivals hall. We also offer curbside pickup upon request.
How much waiting time is included for IAH pickups?▼
We include 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time from the moment your flight lands for domestic flights and 90 minutes for international flights. This covers deplaning, customs, and baggage claim.
How long does it take from Sugar Land to IAH?▼
Travel time from Sugar Land to IAH is approximately 45-60 minutes without traffic. We recommend booking pickup 3 hours before domestic flights and 4 hours before international flights.
Do you offer child car seats for airport transfers?▼
Yes, we provide car seats upon request at no extra charge. Please specify the number and age of children when booking so we can ensure proper installation before arrival.
Can I book a round trip IAH transfer?▼
Absolutely. We recommend booking round-trip service when you book your departure. Your return pickup will be scheduled based on your flight information, and we'll monitor your return flight just like your outbound trip.
