VertigoFix — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

VertigoFix ("the app") is a self-help guide for vertigo-relief exercises, published by Adam J. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

None. VertigoFix does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your symptom logs, exercise streaks, and reminder preferences locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

The app may request notification permission so it can send the exercise reminders you set up. You can decline or revoke this at any time in your device settings.

Videos

Exercise demonstration videos are provided by YouTube. When you choose to play a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that playback.

Medical Disclaimer

VertigoFix is a self-help guide, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Consult a healthcare professional for persistent or severe symptoms.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
